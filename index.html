Stop Chasing the Market

The goal is not to enter simply because an engulfing candle appears. The process is to identify the setup, wait for the retracement, then look for confirmation on the 5-minute chart.

The basic idea: 1H setup → market moves → retracement/pullback → M5 confirmation → entry.

The 5-Minute Entry Process

A simple visual framework for understanding the method.

1

Find the 1H Engulfing

Identify a valid bullish or bearish engulfing candle on the 1-hour timeframe.

2

Let Price Move

Do not rush into the trade. Allow the market to show its reaction.

3

Wait for Retracement

Watch for price to pull back toward the area of interest.

4

Move to M5

Use the 5-minute chart to observe the retracement and look for confirmation.

5

Look for Entry

After confirmation, plan the trade with defined risk and a logical stop-loss.
